## 1. Visual Standard & Production Benchmark Directive

The official character roster of *Ultimate Ninja STORM CONNECTIONS* establishes the non-negotiable benchmark for all character models in *NARUTO: ECHOES OF SHINOBI*. Every character must achieve:
1. **Anatomical Proportions:** Authentic head-to-body ratios (~1:5.3 for Genin/Boyhood, ~1:6.5 for Shippuden/Adults, ~1:7.5 for Divine Otsutsuki).
2. **True 3D Geometry:** Multi-layered hair clumps, modeled hitai-ate plates with corner rivets and stamped village insignias, upright collars with functional zipper sliders, bandages, and sculpted exposed toes on sandals.
3. **Living Facial Rigging:** Modeled almond eye planes, authentic dojutsu (Sharingan 1-3 Tomoe, Mangekyo, Rinnegan, Byakugan, Jogan, Tenseigan), whiskers, teeth rows with black oral depth, and blend shapes for expressions.
4. **Unified Anime Cel Shading:** 2-3 tone stepped cel response driven by `M_AnimeCel_Master` with soft shadow floors ($\ge 0.15$) preventing crushed black silhouettes, coupled with 1.0px dark anime ink contour outlines.

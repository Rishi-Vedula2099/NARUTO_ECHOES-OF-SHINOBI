## 1. Strict Age & Continuity Rules for Cosmetic Skins

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Non-Negotiable Policy: Protection of Underage Characters**
> - Underage characters (e.g., Genin era Naruto, Sasuke, Sakura, Hinata, Neji, Rock Lee) must **NEVER** be sexualized in concept art, 3D models, textures, or cosmetic skins.
> - Cosmetic skins for young shinobi must follow athletic, heroic, traditional cultural, holiday festive (happi/yukata), tactical camouflage, or winter adventure themes.
> - Fashion/glamour skins are strictly restricted to canonical adult character eras (e.g., Adult Hokage era, The Last era, Sannin era) and must maintain dignified aesthetic excellence.

## 1. Colossal Summon: Chief Toad Gamabunta (Mount Myoboku)

### 1.1 Master Summon Visual Prompt

```text
CREATURE PRODUCTION PROMPT — GAMABUNTA (CHIEF TOAD)

FULL IMAGE-GENERATION PROMPT:
CHARACTER: Gamabunta (Chief Toad of Mount Myoboku)
SCALE: Colossal Mega-Beast (Height: 17 meters / 1700 cm)
FACTION: Mount Myoboku / Naruto Contract Summon
ROLE: Colossal Raid Ally / Giant Boss Combatant

ANATOMY & TEXTURE:
A titanic rust-red Japanese toad (Bufo japonicus form) with massive powerful legs, muscular chest, and broad flattened amphibious head. Rough leathery skin covered in irregular dark reddish-brown warty nodules and dark circular spots with fine cel-shaded stippling. A deep, jagged scar cuts across his left eye, which is closed in a permanent wince. Right eye is a massive luminous amber toad eye with horizontal rectangular black slit pupil.

COSTUME & PROPS:
Wears an enormous dark navy-blue happi coat with thick white collar trim and a circular white kanji crest for "Shrine" (蝦) on the back, draped loosely over colossal shoulders. Smokes a giant traditional wooden Japanese kiseru pipe held between jaw lips, from which thick billows of white aromatic tobacco smoke plume upward into the sky. A massive steel dosu short-sword with a braided cord hilt rests in a sheath across his left hip.

VFX & SUMMON ARRIVAL:
Spawns inside a titanic radial explosion of dense white summoning smoke (Niagara particle burst, radius: 2500cm) accompanied by a ground shockwave that lifts dust and small boulders into the air.

POSE: Crouched in an imposing mountain-shaking stance, smoking pipe, glaring downward with one eye at tiny ground opponents.
CAMERA: Ground-level extreme low-angle perspective looking straight up into the towering silhouette of the Chief Toad, 18mm ultra-wide lens.
LIGHTING: Atmospheric daylight filtered through towering plumes of smoke, dramatic sunlight catching the red warty skin highlights.
ENVIRONMENT: Cracked battlefield valley or forest clearing where trees look like tiny matchsticks beneath his webbed feet.
RENDERING: Stylized 3D anime cel-shaded rendering, massive sense of scale, clean ink contours.
```
